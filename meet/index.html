Meet the Author

Request a free visit with the author: a 30-minute interview with Jonathan Lee Davis, in person, virtual, or as a book club chat. Public schools, private schools, and colleges can ask.

Use the form to request a free visit. It opens an email in your own mail app. This site does not store the request. The message is sent only if you send it from your mail app.

Who the visit is for

The visit is free. It is 30 minutes. You can ask for it in person, virtual, or as a book club chat. Public schools, private schools, and colleges can ask. A book club uses the book club chat. The form asks for your name, email, an optional organization, the visit type, a preferred date and time, an optional second choice, and an optional message.

Bring a question about the books you have actually read on this site. The text you can read now is the back-cover description. The books are not released yet. ISBN and release date for each book: TBA. Recommended age or grade level: TBA.

The books you can ask about

Jonathan Lee Davis is the author of the trilogy. Read the back-cover description of New Constellation, the back-cover description of Circle of Stars, and the back-cover description of The Last Constellation. The back covers call the books fiction.

The research behind the books includes interviews, hundreds of hours in libraries and museums, and time with Native American heritage experts. He has been writing these books for 10 years. The stories weave in his life experiences and the people he knew growing up in Arkansas.

Before a class visit, teachers can look at the discussion questions for teachers and professors. Students can look at the student notes on the three novels. A club can look at the book club notes for the trilogy. The descriptions include families leaving home, civil conflict, and war. Read them and decide whether a visit fits.

For a note that is not a visit request, use the contact form. That form also opens an email, and this site does not store the message.
