About the Author

Author photo coming soon

Jonathan Lee Davis is the author of the New Constellation trilogy: New Constellation, Circle of Stars, and The Last Constellation. They are novels. The back covers call the books fiction. ISBN and release date for each book: TBA. Copyright 2026.

He was born in Benton, Arkansas. He attended several elementary and junior high schools across Arkansas, and he graduated from Sheridan High School.

He attended the University of Central Arkansas, then graduated from Arkansas Tech University with a degree in sociology and a minor in military science. He served as an active-duty officer in the U.S. Army and retired from the Army, then became a technology consultant. He attended graduate school at Harvard University.

He is Catholic, a husband, and the father of four daughters and a son.

He has been writing these books for 10 years. The stories weave in his life experiences and the people he knew growing up in Arkansas.

Research behind the books

The research behind the trilogy includes interviews, hundreds of hours in libraries and museums, and time with Native American heritage experts. The novels stay fiction. Each book page has the front cover, the back cover, and the full back-cover description.

The three novels

Read them in order. Each page has the front cover, the back cover, and the full back-cover description.

  • New Constellation, the first novel. The American Dream wore thin. Then the letters arrived. Native American tribes invite back any U.S. citizen with one-eighth Native blood: a home, healthcare, school, honest work, and Sundays kept for church, if the person will live their way.
  • Circle of Stars, the second novel. The line held. Now it has to become a nation. A new flag of thirteen stars flies over a new border while the older country comes apart.
  • The Last Constellation, the third novel. The treaty is signed. Now the war comes home. Thirteen tribes, one border, and a promise never to attack unless attacked.

Book clubs, students, and teachers

Groups can start from the same descriptions. Book club notes for the trilogy collect the three books and explain the free visit. Student notes on the three novels say the books are not released yet and that a recommended age or grade level is TBA. Discussion questions for teachers and professors are templates for public schools, private schools, and colleges. They are a place to start a conversation, not a finished lesson plan.

The descriptions include families leaving home, civil conflict, and war. Read the overview on each book page and decide whether it fits.

Public schools, private schools, and colleges can request a free visit with the author: a 30-minute interview, in person, virtual, or as a book club chat. The form opens an email. This site does not store the request. For other notes about the books, use the contact form.
