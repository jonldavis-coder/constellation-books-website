About the Author

Jonathan Lee Davis is the author of the New Constellation trilogy: New Constellation, Circle of Stars, and The Last Constellation. They are novels. The back covers call the books fiction.

He was born in Benton, Arkansas. He went to Angie Grant Elementary, Perryville Elementary, Perryville Middle School, Benton Middle School, and Sheridan Junior High, and he graduated from Sheridan High School.

He attended the University of Central Arkansas, then graduated from Arkansas Tech University with a degree in sociology and a minor in military science. He served as an active-duty officer in the U.S. Army and retired from the Army, then became a technology consultant. He attended graduate school at Harvard University.

He is Catholic, a husband, and the father of four daughters and a son.

He has been writing these books for 10 years. The stories weave in his life experiences and the people he knew growing up in Arkansas.
