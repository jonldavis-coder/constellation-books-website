Book Clubs

The New Constellation trilogy is three novels by Jonathan Lee Davis: New Constellation, Circle of Stars, and The Last Constellation. The back covers call the books fiction. ISBN and release date for each book: TBA.

Each book page has the full back-cover description. Teacher & Professor Resources also has discussion-question templates drawn from those descriptions, written for public-school, private-school, and college groups.
