Students

Jonathan Lee Davis is the author of the New Constellation trilogy. The three novels, in order, are the back-cover description of New Constellation, the back-cover description of Circle of Stars, and the back-cover description of The Last Constellation.

The books are not released yet. Release dates are TBA. Recommended age or grade level: TBA. The descriptions include families leaving home, civil conflict, and war. Read each overview on the book pages and in the discussion questions for teachers and professors, and decide whether it fits.

What you can read now

This site does not post a sample chapter. The text you can read today is the back-cover description. The back covers call the books fiction. ISBN for each book: TBA. Copyright 2026.

  • Book one: the American Dream has worn thin. Native American tribes invite back any U.S. citizen with one-eighth Native blood: a home, healthcare, school, honest work, and Sundays kept for church, if the person will live their way.
  • Book two: thirteen stars sit in a circle so that no one stood above another, and a new flag flies over a new border while the older country is at war with itself.
  • Book three: the first pattern of stars broke. Thirteen tribes and one border face a war that has come home. The cover names a women's year of service, medicine running short, and gates filling.

The research behind the books includes interviews, hundreds of hours in libraries and museums, and time with Native American heritage experts. The stories weave in his life experiences and the people he knew growing up in Arkansas. He has been writing these books for 10 years.

Your class can start from the discussion questions for teachers and professors. They are templates, not a finished lesson plan. A book club can use the book club notes for the trilogy. Printable guides are marked coming soon on the teacher page. They are not posted yet.
