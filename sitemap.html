The trilogy

Home introduces the New Constellation trilogy and the three novels, in order.

The back-cover description of New Constellation is book one. The American Dream wore thin. Then the letters arrived. Native American tribes invite back any U.S. citizen with one-eighth Native blood.

The back-cover description of Circle of Stars is book two. The line held. Now it has to become a nation. Thirteen stars were set in a circle so that no one stood above another.

The back-cover description of The Last Constellation is book three. The treaty is signed. Now the war comes home.

The author

About Jonathan Lee Davis. He was born in Benton, Arkansas. He attended several elementary and junior high schools across Arkansas, and he graduated from Sheridan High School. He attended the University of Central Arkansas, then graduated from Arkansas Tech University with a degree in sociology and a minor in military science. He served as an active-duty officer in the U.S. Army and retired from the Army, then became a technology consultant. He attended graduate school at Harvard University.

He has been writing these books for 10 years. The stories weave in his life experiences and the people he knew growing up in Arkansas. The research behind the books includes interviews, hundreds of hours in libraries and museums, and time with Native American heritage experts.
