A trilogy of novels

New Constellation

Jonathan Lee Davis is the author of this trilogy. The books, in order, are New Constellation, Circle of Stars, and The Last Constellation.

The back covers call the books fiction. ISBN and release date for each book: TBA.

The three books

Book One of the New Constellation Trilogy

New Constellation

The American Dream wore thin. Then the letters arrived.

When the first American flag flew with thirteen stars, they called the young country a new constellation.
