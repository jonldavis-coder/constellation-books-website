About the author

Jonathan Lee Davis is the author of the New Constellation trilogy. He has been writing these books for 10 years. The stories weave in his life experiences and the people he knew growing up in Arkansas. The research behind the books includes interviews, hundreds of hours in libraries and museums, and time with Native American heritage experts.
