Description

When the first American flag flew with thirteen stars, they called the young country a new constellation. Two and a half centuries later, the American Dream has worn thin. Wages don't stretch, and families wonder what they gave up just to stay afloat.

Then the letters start arriving. Native American tribes are inviting back any U.S. citizen with one-eighth Native blood: a home, healthcare, school, honest work, and Sundays kept for church. All they ask is that you live their way. Thirteen tribes rise across the land. The Supreme Court returns unoccupied government land to them. And a nation that once took everything faces the one thing it never planned for: its own people choosing to leave.

Is it a community, or an army? Is it a refuge, or too good to be true? And once you join, can you ever leave?
